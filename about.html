CONFERENCE PAPER — IBCAST 2026

"A Comparative Evaluation of Large Language Models for Accessibility Barrier Detection in Video Game Reviews"

Authors: K. Batool, F. Gillani, and M. A. Jaffer

Proceedings of the International Bhurban Conference on Applied Sciences and Technologies (IBCAST), 2026.

Abstract: Investigates and benchmarks state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) in automating the extraction and categorization of accessibility barriers from unstructured video game user feedback. The research demonstrates how LLM prompt engineering and fine-tuned classifiers can empower game developers to discover motor, visual, and cognitive hurdles faced by disabled gamers.
